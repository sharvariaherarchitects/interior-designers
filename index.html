The Studio & Philosophy

Bridging Classical Heritage
with Contemporary Poise

Founded in Knightsbridge over two decades ago, Aurelia Sterling is an internationally acclaimed interior architecture and design practice celebrated for crafting deeply personal, collectible residences.

Rejecting transient trends, our multidisciplinary team of architects, interior designers, and master artisans collaborates with European stone quarries, Venetian glassblowers, and British cabinetmakers to realize spaces of enduring warmth and quiet grandeur.
